We engineer metasurfaces and other 2D materials for various applications ranging from reflectivity control to selective spectral absorption. We have developed expertise in numerical design and characterization, micro and nano fabrication optimization under complex constraints, and tunability.

We explore the physics interface in multi-dynamics systems, such as plasma formation for fusion applications or integration on uncommon substrates.
