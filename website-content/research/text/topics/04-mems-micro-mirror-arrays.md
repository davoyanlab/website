We design, fabricate, and experimentally study MEMS (Microelectromechanical Systems) mirror arrays. Our platform consists of centimeter-scale pixels composed of arrays of 100 µm micromirror blades. These pixels are electrostatically actuated in real time, enabling dynamic control of surface properties such as reflectivity and thermal emissivity.

We propose a broadband, fast-response architecture that enables an efficient transition between transparent and reflective states. This approach opens the door to a wide range of applications, from radiation pressure control in spacecraft to tunable emissivity coatings for urban environments and emerging climate challenges.
