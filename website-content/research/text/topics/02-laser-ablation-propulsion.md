Understanding how light interacts with nanostructured materials under extreme conditions, with the aim of enabling new ways to control and utilize optical energy. The work explores nonlinear nanophotonics, where intense light fields drive materials beyond conventional regimes, allowing phenomena such as thermal tuning of optical responses and ultrafast modulation.

This includes laser ablation of structured targets, examining how nanoscale geometry influences energy deposition, material removal, and plasma formation. By linking these processes to broader high-energy physics, the research connects nanoscale light-matter interactions to applications such as laser-driven propulsion and advanced photonic technologies.
