Artur Davoyan is an Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace
Engineering at UCLA. His group works on micro and nano engineering of materials and systems
for high-energy and high-power applications across space exploration, fusion research, and
active materials. The lab is pioneering new concepts of in-space propulsion, such as laser
ablation, laser sailing, and pellet beam propulsion, as well as MEMS devices for optical and
thermal control. Davoyan's work has been recognized through NASA NIAC Fellowships and the
NASA Early Career Faculty award.
